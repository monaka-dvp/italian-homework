The contracted-stem family (G-017) — futuro / condizionale only

These verbs don't use the full infinitive as their stem — the stem shrinks, then takes the normal short endings (-ò -ai -à -emo -ete -anno / -ei -esti -ebbe …). dovere is today's miss — it follows the exact same pattern as andare and venire, already solid.

These are the exceptions. Every regular -are verb builds its condizionale mechanically instead — drop -are, add er, add the ending: compr · er · emmo → compreremmo. That er keeps falling out of the long endings (compremmo, 2026-07-25 and again 2026-07-26), so it now has its own drill: cinque tempi — futuro, imperfetto, condizionale presente, congiuntivo presente, congiuntivo imperfetto — across 40 verbs. Verb, tense and person are all multi-select: take one verb, or a whole spelling family (-care/-gare in one tap), or the lot → coniugazioni.

Congiuntivo presente — beyond avere

Triggered by penso/credo/spero/sono contento che… ([[G-002]]) — the mistake that keeps recurring is memorizing ONE conjugated answer (e.g. siano) and reusing it regardless of person or verb. The trigger is what matters; the ending still has to match the actual subject.

Regular verbs — the ending pattern (nothing to memorize, it's mechanical): -are verbs swap their a for an i → -i -i -i -iamo -iate -ino. -ere / -ire verbs do the mirror image, taking an a → -a -a -a -iamo -iate -ano. Note the two middle rows (noi, voi) are -iamo / -iate for every verb in the language — learn those once and they're done.

The trap that makes the right answer feel wrong (learner's own remark, 2026-07-25: "conjugation of -are verb, I don't know this"): for -are verbs the lui/lei congiuntivo is parli — spelled exactly like the ordinary tu indicative "you speak." So writing Spero che la maestra parli looks like a person error, and the ear reaches for parla instead. It isn't an error: after a trigger, the top three rows are all the same form. If it feels wrong, that's the sign you've got it right.
-care / -gare keep the hard sound with an h — giocare → giochino, pagare → paghi. Same h, same reason, as the conditional giocherei / pagherei in the cards above: c and g are hard before a o u, soft before e i — and every congiuntivo ending here starts with i.
Real misses on record. spero che tu ___ (trovare) → wrote trovate (indicative) instead of troviate — one letter, -a- vs -i- (2026-07-21; fixed cold on 2026-07-25, along with voi venire → veniate). Spero che la maestra ___ (parlare) → wrote parla instead of parli (2026-07-25) — the lui/lei trap above.
The one that was open — and what the three tries revealed. The lui/lei -are slot missed three times, and the interesting part is that it missed differently each time: parla (2026-07-25) → Trovia (2026-07-25 homework) → aspette (2026-07-26, «Penso che la maestra ___ (aspettare) i genitori fuori»). Three tries, three different wrong vowels. That is not someone who forgot the rule — he knows the ending has to change, and cannot retrieve which. A six-person table answers a question he wasn't asking, which is why the 2026-07-25 table scored 100% the same day and was gone in 48 hours. So the treatment changed on 2026-07-26 to one hook, not a table: io / tu / lui / lei are all the same form, and for -are that form ends in -i. Say the hook, then pick the vowel. Scored 4/4 the same session — but primed, so the cold retest was the only one that would count.
And it counted — 2026-07-27, cold. ✓ «Spero che tuo fratello ___ (tornare)» → torni, hint-free by design: nothing taught earlier that day, nothing in the explain box. costi ✓ and apra ✓ alongside it. G-002 advanced for the first time since 2026-06-29 — the retest this slot had owed since 07-21. The one hook held where the six-person table had not. Nothing here is "still open"; the row is on a normal review interval now, so the next thing to watch for is a relapse, not a first pass.

Congiuntivo imperfetto — no longer just avere

Triggered by a real se ("if"): Se avessi tempo, verrei. Full avere paradigm is in the first card above (avess- stem). Graded beyond it: potessi, sapessi and venissimo (cold, 2026-07-28). essere's foss- is banked but not yet taught (G-022) — meet it in the drill as a 📖 studio step, don't reach for it cold.
If you know the imperfetto, this tense is free. The stem is the imperfetto stem — not the infinitive. parlavo → parlassi · facevo → facessi · dicevo → dicessi · bevevo → bevessi. So fare, dire and bere come out right for free — facessi, dicessi, bevessi, never "fassi"/"dissi"/"bessi" (in this tense — dissi is a real form, but it is passato remoto, which you have not met). Only essere · dare · stare keep a paradigm of their own: fossi · dessi · stessi.
Keep the theme vowel — three families, one vowel each. parlassi · sapessi · venissi. Do not think of it as "cut -re, add -essi" — that shortcut is what produced "venessimo" (G-023). And the short stems stop here: potr-, sapr-, avr-, dovr-, andr-, verr- live in exactly two tenses, futuro and condizionale — so potessi, never "potressi". Bonus: no spelling machinery at all in this tense — every ending starts with a consonant, so giocassi and mangiassi need no h and no dropped i, and capissi takes no -isc-.
Which verbs are confirmed, right now: don't trust a list on this page — it went stale once already, still saying "only avere" a day after the class-wide teach. The drill is the live answer: coniugazioni carries congiuntivo imperfetto for every verb and marks each cell's evidence (● drilled · ○ pattern · unmarked = not yet). An unmarked cell isn't forbidden — its first visit is an ungraded 📖 studio step, which is exactly how you're meant to meet a new one.

Imperfetto — hidden-stem verbs

A few verbs keep an old, longer stem in the imperfetto instead of the expected infinitive-minus-ending — then take the normal -vo -vi -va -vamo -vate -vano tail. essere is separately, fully irregular (no -vo tail at all).

But that spot-check reset the row (2026-07-28, G-011 🔁), and the miss has moved. It is no longer the stem: bere → bevuta (the participle drawer opened instead of the imperfetto) and dire → diceve (right stem dic-, wrong ending). So the open front is the lui/lei ending — diceva, beveva — and the participle reflex on bere. bere is now assigned to the coniugazioni drill for exactly this.

Reflexive present tense

The clitic (mi/ti/si/ci/vi/si) sits BEFORE a conjugated verb, or welds onto the back of an infinitive ([[S-006]], devo alzarmi). The verb ending itself is completely normal for its class — only the front pronoun is "reflexive."
