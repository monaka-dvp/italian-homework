V-016/V-017/V-018 🔁 — the survival frames, as WHOLE units

Why these look different from every other card here: Aspetta, ci provo reset twice (2026-07-21, 2026-07-25), so per the stability ladder the treatment changes rather than repeating harder. Every failed attempt was a rebuild from parts — "aspetti ci prova" → "avrò provare" → "Io provo" — so these drill the phrase as one unstructured block: situation on the front, the exact string on the back, no grammar to reassemble under pressure. This is your repair kit: the point is that a missing sentence becomes a move, not a freeze.
SituationTarget sentence
Your daughter asks you something tricky in Italian and waits. Tell her to hold on — you're going to give it a go.Aspetta, ci provo.
A shop assistant asks you something and waits. Tell them to hold on — you're going to give it a go. (someone you address as Lei)Aspetti, ci provo.
Mid-sentence, the word you need just won't come. Say so — the fixed phrase, not an excuse.Non mi viene la parola.
You point at an object whose Italian name you've never learned. Ask for the word itself.Come si dice… in italiano?
You're mid-sentence with another parent and the word vanishes. Say both moves in order — the word won't come, and you'll try anyway.Non mi viene la parola. Aspetta, ci provo in italiano.
A shop assistant is waiting while the word escapes you. Say the word won't come, then that you'll try in Italian. (Lei)Non mi viene la parola. Aspetti, ci provo in italiano.
The word is gone and you need two seconds. Say only the second half — claim the attempt, nothing else.Aspetta, ci provo in italiano.

V-024 — "I've heard it's beautiful"

2026-07-25 (L018): taught 07-21, missed again on the homework comeback — "Ho sento che Sardegna é belissimo". Three slips in one short sentence: the participle (ho sentito, not ho sento), the article a region name needs (la Sardegna), and the agreement (bellissima, feminine, double l). It had no card of its own until now.
SituationTarget sentence
The visiting parents say they're spending two weeks in Sardinia. Say you've heard it's gorgeous — name the place.Ho sentito che la Sardegna è bellissima.
A parent says they're off to Napoli. Say you've heard it's gorgeous — without repeating the place name.Ho sentito che è bellissima.

V-025 / V-026 — restaurant + bar (the 2026-07-25 Dillo capture)

Both deliberately generalized so they work anywhere: il mio piatto ("my dish") saves you from ever needing the specific food word, and in tutto ("in total") works at any till, for any count of anything. Note arrivare takes essere, so the participle agrees with the thing that hasn't arrived.
SituationTarget sentence
At the restaurant everyone is eating and your dish still hasn't come. Get the waiter's attention and tell him. (Lei)Scusi, il mio piatto non è ancora arrivato.
At the restaurant your wife's pizza still hasn't come. Tell the waiter. (Lei — the ending agrees with pizza)Scusi, la sua pizza non è ancora arrivata.
Your pasta still hasn't come. Tell the waiter using mancare ('to be missing') rather than 'arrive'. (Lei)Scusi, manca ancora la pasta.
At the bar the barista asks «Allora, cosa prendete?» — you want two coffees and nothing else. Give him the total.Due caffè in tutto.
You've ordered a few things at the bar and want the price for the lot. Ask.Quant'è in tutto?
